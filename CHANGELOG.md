## [1.0.0] - 2025-08-07

### Added
- Initial release of `nextvikas/laravel-google-authenticator` package.
- Supports Google Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) for Laravel applications.
- Compatible with Laravel 7 to 12 and PHP 7.4 to 8.3.
- Middleware for OTP verification and setup.
- Configurable routes, guards, and secret column.
- Publishable config, views, and migration files.
- `AuthenticatorServiceProvider` for easy bootstrapping.
